About us

Rooted in Jamkhed.

Mauli Sliding and Glass Arts is run by Pradip (Mauli) Mule on Beed Road, Jamkhed. Since 2023, we have been making windows, doors, partitions, mirrors and railings for local homes, shops and offices.

  • Right here in Jamkhed
